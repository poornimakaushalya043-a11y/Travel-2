Plan With Confidence

Essential Island Travel Wisdom

Thoughtful preparation ensures seamless journeys across Sri Lanka’s diverse microclimates and heritage sites.

🛂

Visas & Entry

Verify current Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) and passport validity requirements via official Sri Lankan immigration portals prior to departure.

🚂

Scenic Transit

Reserve hill-country train seats (Kandy to Ella) weeks ahead. For flexible multi-stop routes, private drivers or coastal expressways save hours.

🌤️

Two-Season Sun

Dec–Apr brings sunshine to the South & West coasts; May–Sep illuminates the East coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay, Pasikudah).

🌿

Sacred Etiquette

Cover shoulders and knees and remove footwear at temples. Maintain respectful distances during wildlife safaris and avoid single-use plastics.
